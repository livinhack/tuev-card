# b179 – Reduced short top row centered in fixed three-letter field

The standalone Physical Lab corrects the reduced two-line standard template for short upper district rows.

## Background

- b176/b177 placed short upper rows too far to the right.
- b178 filled visible letters from slot 1 toward slot 3, which placed short upper rows too far to the left.
- External examples from registration sites indicate that 1–2 district letters are centered inside the fixed three-letter field.

## Rule

For reduced two-line standard plates only:

- 3 upper letters fill the fixed three-letter area normally.
- 2 upper letters are centered within that fixed three-letter area.
- 1 upper letter is centered within that fixed three-letter area.
- Missing slots are virtual placeholders.
- The right-side seal/width template remains governed by the reduced standard template and the lower row.

## Scope

- Reduced standard only.
- Reduced H/E, season and green remain disabled.
- No Card renderer integration.
- b172 remains rejected.
- b170 remains the confirmed motorcycle checkpoint.
