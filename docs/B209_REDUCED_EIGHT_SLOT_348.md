This Full handover documents the separate Physical Lab b209. Card code was not changed.

## Lab change

For Reduced two-line H/E/Saison cases with 8 occupied slots, the upper side-by-side seal row now uses:

```text
Text → authority seal = 3 mm
authority seal → HU = 4 mm
right outside margin >= 8 mm
```

The existing 9-slot season tight case remains separate with right outside margin `>= 6 mm`.
