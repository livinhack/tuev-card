# b120 – Two-line height geometry alignment

b120 corrects the first two-line Physical Lab geometry against the Anlage 4 reference sketch.

## What changed

The two-line plate now uses the reference vertical split inside the 200 mm outer height:

- outer height: 200 mm
- border / inner inset: 4.5 mm top and bottom
- inner height: 191 mm
- Euro field: y 4.5 mm, height 88 mm
- top character band: y 17.5 mm, height 75 mm
- row gap: 15 mm
- bottom character band: y 107.5 mm, height 75 mm
- remaining inner bottom margin: 13 mm

The former b119 two-line start values used top row y 14 mm and bottom row y 111 mm. That kept the 75 mm row height but did not match the 13 / 75 / 15 / 75 / 13 mm reference split.

## Seal positions

The neutral two-line seal placeholders were moved with the corrected top row geometry:

- HU circle center y: 35 mm
- authority seal center y: 78 mm

The circles keep their visible 35 mm / 45 mm diameters.

## Scope

This is still Lab-only for the two-line format. The production Card remains on the established one-line physical renderer until the two-line Lab model is visually approved.
