## Reduced standard rules captured in b174

- Outer size: `255 × 130 mm`.
- Euro field: `35 × 56 mm`.
- Euro raster: `5 / 22.5 / 8 / 15 / 5.5 mm`.
- Top and bottom text bands: `49 mm`.
- Inter-row gap: `12 mm`.
- Letters: `31 mm`.
- Digits: `29 mm`.
- `* = at least 8 mm`.
- `** = 8–10 mm`, currently template-preferred `9 mm`.
- `*** = 5–20 mm`, currently template-preferred `15 mm`.
- Bottom letter-to-digit group gap uses `15–18 mm`, currently `15 mm`.
- HU and authority seal placeholders are x-aligned in one vertical seal column.
- Both rows reserve the same seal column; the lower text no longer overlaps the authority seal.
